Frequently Asked Questions

How do AI receptionists work?

An AI receptionist answers calls your team misses, using natural voice AI to have a real conversation with the caller — capturing their name, need, and contact info — then instantly texts you the details so you can follow up. NeverMissLeadsNow only picks up when a call goes unanswered (busy, after 3 rings, or your phone's off), so it's backing you up, not replacing you.

What is an AI receptionist?

It's a voice AI system that answers phone calls on your business's behalf when you can't get to them, sounding like a real person rather than a robotic menu. NeverMissLeadsNow is built specifically for contractors and home-service businesses: it introduces itself with your business name, asks what the caller needs, and captures the lead instead of letting the call go to voicemail — or nowhere.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

NeverMissLeadsNow starts at $44/month for 300 AI-answered minutes, with Growth ($144/mo, 500 minutes) and Pro ($294/mo, 1,500 minutes) above that. Every plan includes a free 7-day trial, no contract, and cancel anytime — no hidden fees.

Are AI receptionists worth it?

For a business that loses jobs to unanswered calls, yes — most missed calls simply never get returned; the caller just moves to the next name on the list. An AI receptionist's job is narrow but high-value: pick up the calls you'd otherwise miss and get the lead's info before they hang up. It's worth it for that specific gap, not as a replacement for how you run your business.

Which AI virtual receptionist is best for small businesses?

It depends on the business type. NeverMissLeadsNow is built specifically for solo and small-team home-service contractors — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, and similar trades — not general office reception, so the setup, pricing, and the assistant's phone script are all built around a contractor's own missed calls, not a multi-department office switchboard.

Can an AI receptionist integrate with my CRM?

Not yet as a built-in feature. Every captured lead is saved and you get an instant SMS with the caller's details, which most solo contractors use directly instead of a CRM. If you run a CRM and need leads wired into it, reach out — that's not automated today.

Will AI replace receptionists?

For solo contractors and small crews, there was rarely a dedicated receptionist to replace — the "receptionist" was often just whoever could grab the phone, or nobody, while the crew was mid-job. An AI receptionist isn't replacing a role here; it's filling a gap that never had staffing to begin with: someone to answer when you physically can't.
